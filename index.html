Proactive Knowledge Base & SOP Redesign

Improving operational workflows, eliminating friction points, and reducing unnecessary support escalations.

Optimization 1: Liquor Stores POS Integration Workflow Redesign

The Operational Gap: Previous SOP required agents to ask merchants for their POS provider name first before checking if the store was managed by an Account Manager. If an account had no Account Manager (platform employee managing the store), integration could not be offered regardless of the POS provider. This caused prolonged interactions and negative merchant experiences.

The Solution & Action Taken: Updated the SOP and filed Jira requests to reorder the verification steps. Agents now verify Account Manager status as the mandatory first gatekeeper. If unmanaged, integration is declined immediately before requesting POS details, saving time and eliminating frustration.

Optimization 2: POS Delivery Configuration & Root Cause Routing

The Operational Gap: Merchants with POS integrations experiencing unaccepted orders while configured for "delivery by own staff" were previously routed into unnecessary Jira tickets. The root cause stemmed from incompatible POS provider configurations that failed to support self-staff delivery modes.

The Solution & Action Taken: Updated internal KB documentation to instantly identify POS delivery incompatibilities. Instead of filing redundant Jira tickets, agents now inform merchants immediately and provide 2 clear alternatives: either switch to a compatible POS provider or update their delivery mode settings.
